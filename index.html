I honestly don't know how to begin this without sounding too nervous, but I guess that's what happens when you're finally about to say something you've kept inside for quite some time.

I've been thinking about whether I should tell you this or just let these feelings remain a little secret of mine. I've imagined different possibilities, different conversations, and even different ways of saying everything. But somehow, none of them seemed enough to explain what I genuinely feel.

So, I'm choosing to be honest.

I like you.

And it's not just because of how you look or the way you make people smile. It's something about you as a person. The little things you do, the way you express yourself, the way you tell stories, the random conversations we have, and even those moments when we don't really have anything important to talk about.

Somehow, those ordinary moments have become something I look forward to.

I didn't plan on developing feelings for you. There wasn't some particular moment when I suddenly decided that I wanted you to be someone special. It happened gradually. Somewhere between our conversations, the little interactions, and the moments I found myself smiling because of you, I realized that you had become someone I genuinely cared about.

I started wondering how your day was going. I found myself wanting to hear about the things that made you happy, the things that frustrated you, and even the random little details you probably wouldn't think twice about sharing.

And perhaps that's when I understood that this wasn't just a passing feeling anymore.

I want you to know that I'm not confessing because I expect something in return. I don't want you to feel obligated to like me just because I decided to be honest about my feelings.

I know that feelings aren't something we can force. They take time, understanding, and a genuine willingness from both people. And if there's anything I want you to feel while reading this, it's that you are free to be honest with me too.

I'm aware that this might change the way we interact with each other. That's probably one of the reasons why I hesitated for so long. I value the connection we have, and I wouldn't want one confession to make you feel uncomfortable or make you think that you owe me something.

I don't need an immediate answer. I don't need you to figure everything out right now. Take your time. Think about it. And most importantly, listen to what you genuinely feel.

But if there's a chance that you might feel something for me too, I'd be more than willing to get to know you on a deeper level. I'd love to create memories with you, discover more about who you are, and see where this little unexpected feeling could possibly lead us.

I can't promise that everything will always be perfect. But I can promise that my intentions are genuine. I want something that grows naturally, something built on honesty, respect, patience, and the willingness to understand one another.

And if things don't turn out the way I secretly hope they will, I hope we can still appreciate the fact that I was brave enough to tell you.

Because sometimes, I think the hardest part isn't having feelings for someone. It's finding the courage to let them know.

So here I am, finally saying it instead of wondering what would happen if I did.

I like you. I genuinely do.

And I think you're someone worth taking a chance on.

Whatever your answer may be, thank you for taking the time to read this. You don't know how much courage it took for me to put all of these feelings into words.

No pressure. No expectations. Just honesty.
